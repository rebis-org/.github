# Accessibility

## Scope and precedence

Organization-default accessibility statement for all OSS and FOSS projects, as defined in [On Openness][openness] in the organization profile. A repository's own accessibility statement extends this one with project-specific goals, environments, and barriers; where inconsistent, the repository-specific provisions prevail.

## Goals

- Web properties target [WCAG 2.2][wcag] Level AA.
- Documents and forms aim to remain readable and operable with assistive technology; where a published format presents a barrier, an alternative is provided on request through the private channel.
- Software follows its platform's accessibility conventions where the platform provides them.

These are best-effort goals, not conformance certifications; no project claims conformance unless its own documentation says so.

## Known barriers

No organization-wide barriers are currently on record. A project's known barriers and supported environments are documented in its own accessibility statement where present.

## Reporting a barrier

Report a barrier in organization [Discussions][discussions] with: the repository; the environment, including browser or operating system and the assistive technology in use; and the steps that expose the barrier. Reports involving private information go to contact[at]rebis[dot]cn instead, with a minimal, non-sensitive summary. Barrier reports are triaged as defects.
