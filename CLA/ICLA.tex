\Sec{0}{Parties and scope}

\P{0.1}{"You" means the individual identified in the signature block below, acting in a personal capacity on Your own behalf and not on behalf of any employer or other entity.}

\P{0.2}{All correspondence under this Agreement shall be conducted through a personal email address under Your long-term control. For the avoidance of doubt, no employer-owned address shall be used for such correspondence.}

\P{0.3}{Contributions made on behalf of an employer or other entity are not covered by this Agreement; such contributions require a Corporate CLA ("CCLA") executed by an authorized representative and on file with the Organization. Where contributions in both capacities occur, both agreements shall be on file, and each code offering shall state the capacity in which it is made --- personal, or the entity name under its CCLA.}

\Sec{1}{Definitions}

\P{1.1}{"Contribution" means any original work of authorship, authored by You and intentionally submitted for inclusion in any repository or work maintained by the Organization.}

\P{1.2}{"Submitted" means any form of electronic, verbal, or written communication addressed to the Organization or its representatives, for the purpose of discussing or improving the work, but excluding any communication conspicuously marked or otherwise designated in writing by You as "Not a Contribution."}

\P{1.3}{Third-party material not authored by You, and any of Your own work already subject to explicit licensing terms at the time of submission, are outside the scope of this Agreement and remain governed by their respective terms (see \S5). Only Your original work submitted without explicit licensing terms is licensed under \S\S2--3.}
