# Code of Conduct

## Adoption and precedence

This organization adopts the [Contributor Covenant 3.0][cc] as the organization-default code of conduct for all OSS and FOSS projects, as defined in [On Openness][openness] in the organization profile. Participation in the organization's community spaces constitutes agreement to uphold it.

## Scope

Public repositories, organization [Discussions][discussions], security reports, and private correspondence concerning conduct.

## Enforcement

Report suspected violations to contact[at]rebis[dot]cn with links, timestamps, and context; not in discussion threads. Do not litigate suspected violations in public. Reports are handled confidentially; retaliation against good-faith reporters is itself a violation.
